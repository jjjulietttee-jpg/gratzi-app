The plain version

Use Gratzi to track your own work. Do not copy it, resell it or pick it apart. Your numbers are yours.

What you may do

Install it on iPhones you control and keep your own shift records with it. The app is licensed to you, not sold.

Pro plans and the free week

Some tools, such as the month-end tip report, the goal tracker and extra jobs, sit behind Gratzi Pro, offered as yearly or monthly. The price in your currency and a free introductory period, when you qualify for one, are printed on the screen before you commit.

How renewal works

  • Apple bills your Apple ID at confirmation.
  • Plans renew on their own at the close of each term.
  • To avoid the next charge, switch renewal off at least 24 hours earlier.
  • Charges for a coming term can appear up to a day before it starts.

Ending a plan

Open Settings on the iPhone, tap your name, choose Subscriptions, then Gratzi, and turn renewal off. Inside the app, Settings has a Manage subscription row that jumps to the same page. A refund is Apple’s decision; ask for one on reportaproblem.apple.com.

New phone?

Settings in the app has Restore Purchases. Sign in with the same Apple ID first.

Tips, taxes and the report

The report arranges your own entries in a layout inspired by the monthly tip record that U.S. employees give employers. It is a convenience, not tax, payroll or legal advice, and it is not an official government form. Rules differ by country and state, so confirm figures with your employer or a qualified preparer. Accuracy depends on what you typed.

Limits

The app is supplied as it comes. As far as the law lets us, the publisher is not responsible for missed tips, mistaken tax filings, or data lost with a lost or broken phone. Rights you hold under local consumer law are untouched, and Apple’s standard app licence applies as well.
