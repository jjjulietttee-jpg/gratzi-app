Common questions

How do I enter cents? The keypad works like a register: typing 1 4 2 5 0 gives $142.50. Use “00” for whole dollars.

What is tip-out? The part of your tips you share with bussers, bartenders or runners. Gratzi subtracts it from your take-home and your hourly rate.

How is the real hourly rate counted? (Cash + card tips − tip-out + base wage × hours) ÷ hours.

When does Best shifts appear? After 8 closed shifts. A day and shift needs at least two entries to be ranked.

Restore purchases. Settings in Gratzi → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Cancel a subscription. Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Gratzi.
