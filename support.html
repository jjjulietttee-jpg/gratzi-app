I bought Pro and it shows as locked.

Go to Settings in Gratzi and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure the iPhone uses the Apple ID that paid.

My widget shows old numbers.

Open Gratzi once; closing a shift refreshes the widget summary. Press and hold the widget, then remove and add it again if it lags.

The reminder never fires.

Check that the toggle is on in Settings in the app and that Gratzi has Notifications allowed in iOS Settings. Pick the time again to reschedule it.

How do I fix a wrong entry?

Open the shift from the Ledger and edit it, or swipe it and tap Delete then close the shift again.

How do I get my shifts out?

Settings, then Export all shifts (CSV). For one month with a summary, open the tip report and pick PDF or CSV.

Did my history vanish on a new iPhone?

Gratzi keeps data on the device, so it only moves with a device backup or transfer. We cannot restore it from a server because we have none.

How do I stop the free week?

iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Gratzi. Turn renewal off a day ahead of the end.

Is the tip report a tax form?

No. It lists your numbers in a tidy layout you can hand to an employer. Treat it as a helper.
