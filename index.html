Tips, tallied.

A shift log for servers, bartenders and delivery drivers who want to know what an hour is really worth.

What is Gratzi?

You close a shift in a few taps: hours, cash tips, card tips and what you tipped out. Gratzi keeps the history, works out your true hourly rate and, on Pro, builds a month-end report with the fields tipped workers need for their paperwork.
