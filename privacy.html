Written October 6, 2026. Read as questions, because that is how people ask.

Where do my shifts go?

Nowhere. Jobs, shifts, wages, hours, cash, card, tip-out and notes sit in a database inside the Gratzi app on your iPhone. There is no sign-up and no cloud copy made by us.

Does anything else on my phone read them?

Yes, one thing, and it is ours: the Today widget. The app and its widget share a small summary of the day through an app group, a private space that only apps from this developer can open. It carries the figures the widget prints and nothing else.

Is anything sent to a company?

Only Apple

Gratzi has no analytics kit, no advertising network and no subscription middleman. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple’s StoreKit, so Apple, not us, sees your Apple ID and payment method. Gratzi simply asks the system whether Pro is active.

What about the daily reminder?

If you switch on the close-shift reminder, iOS asks if Gratzi may notify you. The alert is scheduled on the phone for the time you choose and repeats daily. It involves no push server. Turning it off inside the app, or in iOS Settings, cancels it.

How do reports and exports leave the phone?

  • Export PDF or Export CSV in the tip report, and Export all shifts (CSV) in Settings, build a file on the device.
  • The share sheet opens, and the file goes only where you point it.
  • After that, the receiving app or person controls the copy.

Do you track me?

No. Gratzi reads no advertising identifier and does not follow you across other apps or websites.

How do I delete things?

Swipe a shift in the Ledger and press Delete; a note confirms the shift is gone. Jobs can be removed from the Jobs screen. Taking the app off your iPhone erases the lot, including the widget data. We hold no server copy, so nothing remains to be erased by request.

Is it for kids?

No, it is built for working adults; we do not knowingly cater to under-13s.
