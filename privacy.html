Privacy Policy

Gratzi: Server Tip Tracker · Last updated September 25, 2026

Gratzi is a tip tracker for servers, bartenders, baristas and delivery drivers. It is built so that your earnings stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no server that stores your shifts, and we don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

  • Your shifts: date, hours, cash tips, card tips, tip-out, notes.
  • Your jobs, their base wage and usual shift lengths, your monthly goal and reminder time.
  • Reports you export (PDF or CSV) are created on your iPhone and go only where you send them.

Widget

The Today widget reads a small summary (today's and this week's totals) that Gratzi writes to a storage area shared only between the app and its own widget on your iPhone.

Notifications

If you turn on the close-shift reminder, Gratzi schedules a local notification on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Gratzi receives only the status of your purchase, never your payment details. We may use a subscription-management service (Adapty) that receives an anonymous app-install identifier and purchase status to validate purchases; it never receives your shifts or earnings.

Analytics and tracking

Gratzi does not track you across apps or websites and does not use advertising identifiers.

Deleting your data

Delete a shift or a job in the app, or delete Gratzi from your iPhone to remove all of its data. You can export all shifts as CSV from Settings at any time.

Children

Gratzi is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
